What happens to your words

  1. Transcribe — speech becomes text in the language spoken
  2. Identify language — by script and marker words
  3. Redact — phone numbers and IDs stripped before anything is stored
  4. Classify — matched to one of ten development sectors
  5. Extract — urgency and how many people are affected
  6. Score confidence — anything uncertain goes to a human, not into a funding decision
